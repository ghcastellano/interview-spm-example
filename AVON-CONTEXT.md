# Avon business context and role coverage

This is Gustavo Castellano’s example transformation approach using the Northstar dashboard framework. It is not an Avon internal dashboard, audit, approved procedure or claim of implementation results. Existing colors, navigation, table layout and styles are preserved. The presentation-guide controls have been removed for client sharing. The entry page explains the proposed operating approach, clearly labels mock data and describes future connections to the client’s existing management tools (for example Jira, Oracle work-management solutions or other tools), without asserting any product is currently used or connected.

## Public facts used

Reviewed September 29, 2026:

- [Avon home](https://www.avonrents.com/): rentals for production, business and individual customers; multi-vehicle production requests; studio parking.
- [Fleet](https://www.avonrents.com/fleet/): specialty production vehicles, trucks, vans and passenger vehicles inform sample workflow coverage.
- [Locations](https://www.avonrents.com/our-locations/): Hollywood and Saticoy are used as existing-branch pilot labels. The [About page](https://www.avonrents.com/about-us/) describes a different footprint, so this case makes no claim about the company’s total current locations.
- [FAQs](https://www.avonrents.com/faqs/): extensions, pickup/return processes and parking inform the illustrative SOPs. All proposed controls still require business-owner validation.
- [Corporate accounts](https://www.avonrents.com/resources/corporate-accounts/): account documentation, authorization and operational handoffs inform sample billing and training work. No legal or insurance rules are invented.

## Explicit scenario assumptions

The ERP vendor, actual architecture, defects, workarounds, staffing, budgets, project results and operational baselines are unknown. The public website platform is not evidence of an ERP product. No actual Jira or Avon operational data is used.

The job description mentions an incoming managed portfolio; its identity, geography, resource mix and size remain unknown. The mock dataset has 3,000 scoped records: 1,200 for a Hollywood pilot cohort, 1,000 for a Saticoy pilot cohort and 800 hypothetical incoming records. These are not verified fleet sizes or an acquisition announcement. Parking resources and vehicle inventory have separate proposed availability controls.

All owners other than the presenter are fictional. Executive titles are illustrative decision rights, not verified organizational facts. All dates and amounts are scenario values. Snapshot: September 29, 2026. Training languages, cutover windows, recovery objectives and acceptance thresholds require discovery and approval.

## Evidence boundaries

The written SOP examples and interactive register are included. Screenshot packs and video demonstrations are planned deliverables, not supplied system documentation. Project details define proposed outputs, acceptance criteria and operational metrics. They do not invent actual company KPI values.

All mock figures reconcile: 2,902/3,000 records mapped; 98 exceptions (84 duplicate records and 14 incomplete records); 92/120 learners certified. Certification totals reconcile across role paths and rollout cohorts. No rollout group is cleared to launch. Only 2/5 cutover gates pass. Simulated executive approval does not change project status or satisfy evidence gates.
